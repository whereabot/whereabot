# Whereabot brand mark

`mark.svg` is the Whereabot mark: a location pin that holds a bell.
The bell answers when called. The pin shows where you are.
The mark is flat, has no text, and reads at 32 px.

## Colours

- Pin: `#2F5BEA` (primary blue)
- Bell and clapper ring: `#FFFFFF` (neutral white)
- Clapper: `#FFB020` (accent amber)

## Usage

- Use `mark.svg` as is. Do not add text, outlines or effects.
- Keep at least 8 px of clear space around the mark.
- On dark backgrounds, keep the pin colour. Do not swap the bell colour.
- Minimum size: 32 px.
